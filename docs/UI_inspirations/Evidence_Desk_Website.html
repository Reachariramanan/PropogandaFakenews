Research protocol / 04

Methodology

Make the reasoning auditable. Separate retrieval, source assessment, claim comparison, and the final language used to describe uncertainty.

Protocol v0.1

Claim verification pipeline

A suggested system flow for a production implementation.

4 stages
01

Normalize and extract claims

Preserve the original text, identify atomic factual assertions, classify opinion versus checkable statements, and retain spans for traceability. Record language, date, location, and entities where present.

Claim segmentationNER / entity linkingSpan offsets
02

Retrieve evidence with provenance

Search primary documents, reputable reporting, and relevant reference data. Record source URL, publication date, retrieval time, snippet offsets, and the relationship between sources.

Hybrid retrievalSource metadataCitation trace
03

Compare evidence and identify conflict

Assess whether sources support, contradict, contextualize, or do not address each claim. Deduplicate copied reports and distinguish agreement from independent corroboration.

Entailment / contradictionIndependent corroborationTemporal checks
04

Generate a calibrated explanation

Return a label with cited evidence, counterevidence, missing context, and uncertainty. Escalate ambiguous, high-impact, or poorly sourced claims to human review. Do not equate model scores with factual probability without calibration.

Evidence-backed outputCalibrationHuman oversight

Assessment vocabulary

Prefer precise states over one simplistic truth score.

✓
Supported / verifiedReliable evidence supports the central factual claim.
!
Contradicted / misleadingEvidence conflicts with the claim or its framing omits material context.
◷
Insufficient evidenceCurrent retrieval cannot establish a defensible conclusion.
↔
Context dependentMeaning changes with a date, location, definition, or omitted condition.
